Resting HRV, day by day

 

Dots: the single 5-minute windows (spread over the day's slot in time order; click one to open it on the review page). Large dot: the day's median — outside the normal range, the unfavourable or the favourable way (for HR: higher is unfavourable). Line: 7-day rolling mean. Band: normal range = mean ± 0.5 SD of the preceding 30 days (the smallest worthwhile change, Plews et al.); from 5 days of data. A 7-day mean leaving the band is the signal worth acting on; single days scatter.
